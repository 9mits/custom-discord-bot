## Nine Secrets

**Nine new SECRETS** just landed in the crate!
The game will not tell you what a single one of them does.

And getting in is just **Verify** — no form, no waiting.

*Somebody is going to pull #1.*

## Read This First

### ⚠️ Java Needs 1.21.6 Or Newer

**Minecraft 1.21.6 is now the Java minimum!**
The resource pack starts at pack format **63**.
Anything older loads **none** of it — every cosmetic, crate key, and potion shows up as the **wrong item**!

Change your version in the launcher and jump back in!
**Bedrock players** — nothing changes for you.

*Now you can actually see what you own.*

### 🔓 Press Verify

No written form.
No review queue.
No staff approval.

Press **Verify** in Discord, enter your exact username, and **join once**.
You are **let straight in**.
That is the whole gate now!

*The button used to say Apply. It was lying.*

## Featuring

### 🕶️ Nine Secrets

Three secret **Kill Effects**. Three secret **Auras**. Three secret **Trails**.
The crate shows the same **black silhouette** for all nine!
No name. No rarity. No percentage.
Win one and it reveals its real name, icon, and effect everywhere at once!

![](secret-icons.png)

The old Secret category is **gone**.
A secret now sits inside its **real** category.
So you can equip **one from every category at the same time**!

![](equipped.png)

*Nobody has seen these yet. Someone has to be first.*

### 🧪 Fortune & Crate Luck

Two brand-new potions dropped into the crate!

**Fortune Potion I-V** - up to **5x** the drops from ores, Ancient Debris, and crops
**Crate Luck II-V** - up to **5x** the weight on every rare crate reward

Both run for **5 minutes** and both survive a reconnect!

![](potions.png)

*Drink the Crate Luck. Open everything. Do not think about it.*

### ⛏️ Enchantments Beyond Vanilla

**Excavation I** breaks a full **3x3** with a pickaxe!
It is the **rarest book** in the whole crate.
**Unbreaking IV** and **V**!
**Protection V**!
**Fortune IV** and **V**!
Slap them on at an **anvil** like any other book.

Four vanilla potions joined the pool too - **Healing II**, **Strength II**, **Swiftness II**, and **Fire Resistance**.

![](enchantments.png)

*Fortune V sits at 0.050%. Feeling lucky?*

### 🔢 Every Cosmetic Is Numbered

Every cosmetic you pull now gets a **serial number**!
`/wardrobe` shows the serial, the **rarity**, and how many exist.
**Serial #1** is the first one ever pulled, and there is only ever one!

![](serial-number.png)

Cosmetics go on the **Auction House** straight from your wardrobe now.
No physical token needed. Pick it, price it, done!

*Go check your serials. Somebody wants them.*

## Quality of Life

### 💰 Money Under Your Name

Every player's balance now hangs **under their name tag**, short and clean as **M** and **B**!

![](nameplate-money.png)

*Read the room before you start a fight.*

### 📋 A Cleaner Tab List

The tab list finally **lines up**!
Rank, clan, name, edition and ping all sit in real columns.
Your linked **Discord name** shows next to your username too.

![](tablist.png)

### ⏳ Teleport Countdown

Teleports now count down **on your screen**, one second at a time!
Take a hit during the warmup and it **cancels**.

![](teleport-countdown.png)

### 💤 AFK

`/afk` marks you on the tab list!
Sit still for **5 minutes** and the server marks you automatically.
Move a block and you are back.
AFK time still earns **crate keys**.

*Go make a sandwich. The hour still counts.*

### 📢 Broadcasts You Cannot Miss

Server broadcasts land as a **big red bar** across the top of your screen!
The bar **empties as it goes**, so you can see exactly how long is left.
Two announcements at once? You get **two bars**, stacked.

![](broadcast-bar.png)

Every broadcast hits **chat** as well, boxed in red with a **bell**.
Look away from your screen and you will still hear it.

![](broadcast-chat.png)

*No more important news scrolling past while you sort your chests.*
