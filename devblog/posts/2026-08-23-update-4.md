## 2x Key Event

A **2X KEY EVENT** is live right now!

And it is landing on a **brand new base rate** — the hourly key was **upgraded from 1 to 2**.
The event **doubles that**, not the old number.

**2 keys an hour** becomes **4**.
**3 an hour** for boosters becomes **6**.

*Every hour you are logged in during this is worth four of the old ones.*

![](two-keys.png)

## The Crate

### 🔑 The Base Rate Doubled

This part is **permanent**, event or no event.

**2 keys** for every hour online, up from 1.
**3 keys** an hour for **boosters**, up from 2.
Nothing to claim, nothing to click — play and they arrive.

**2x Keys** multiplies whatever your rate is, so the event pays **4** and **6**.

*Your alts are welcome. Macros and auto-clickers are allowed here.*

### 🎰 Every Rare Just Got 45% Rarer

The crate has been **nerfed**, on purpose, everywhere it counts!

**Totem of Undying** — 0.750% → **0.413%**
**Netherite Ingot** — 0.300% → **0.165%**
**Enchanted Golden Apple** — 0.200% → **0.110%**
**Mace** — 0.050% → **0.028%**
**Fortune V** — 0.050% → **0.028%**
**Every secret cosmetic** — 0.005% → **0.003%**

Rare rewards now land on **4.792%** of openings, down from 8.703%.
Twice the keys, half the odds — you open twice as often for about what you were getting before.

*And during the event, four times as often.*

### 🔔 The Chime Means Something Now

That server-wide **DING** used to fire for anything under 1%.
It fired constantly. Nobody looked up.

It is now reserved for **anything rarer than 0.01%**.
Hear it, and someone just pulled something almost nobody will ever see.

*If you hear it twice in one night, go check who.*

### 📦 Rewards Finally Stack

Ten openings used to mean ten separate slots of **2 diamonds**.
Crate rewards now drop straight into the stack you are already carrying!

### 🪵 Copper Is Out

**32 Oak Wood** replaced the copper reward.
The shop sells every log — it does not sell the six-sided **bark block**.

*A reward you could just buy was never a reward.*

## Potions

![](potions.png)

### 🧪 Fortune Potions Cut In Half

Every tier keeps **half the bonus** it used to give!

**Fortune Potion I** — 1.5x → **1.25x**
**II** — 2x → **1.5x**
**III** — 3x → **2x**
**IV** — 4x → **2.5x**
**V** — 5x → **3x**

Still **5 minutes**. Still survives a reconnect.

### 🍀 Crate Luck Cut In Half

**Crate Luck II** — 2x → **1.5x**
**III** — 3x → **2x**
**IV** — 4x → **2.5x**
**V** — 5x → **3x**

The ceiling with a **2x event** running is now **3x**, not 5x.

### ⛏️ Fortune IV And V Toned Down

The books keep **half of what they add** above vanilla Fortune III.
**Fortune IV** now averages **2.43x** ore drops.
**Fortune V** averages **2.67x**.

Stack a **Fortune Potion V** on top of **Fortune V** and the old **15.7x** is now **8x**.

*Still the best pickaxe on the server. Just not a printer.*

## Featuring

**2x Keys** is the one running today — but there are four of them!

**2x Money**

### 💸 /autopay

Set up a payment and forget about it!

`/autopay <player> <amount|all> [seconds]`

Send a fixed amount, or **all** of it, every **30 seconds** by default.
Perfect for funding a clan mate, or a shop that runs itself.

### 💤 AFK Means Untouchable

Sit still for **5 minutes** and the server marks you **AFK** — now mobs, fall damage and lava cannot touch you.

A **player** who hits you does not bounce off — you simply **wake up**.
So `/afk` is not an immunity button mid-fight.
The **void** still kills, as it should.

*Park anywhere. Just not over a hole.*
