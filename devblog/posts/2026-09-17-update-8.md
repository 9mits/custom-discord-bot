## The PvP Islands

⚔️ Competitive PvP is its own **WORLD** now!

![](pvp-islands.png)

Walk through an arch at the hub and you are on that mode's own island. Queue up and you are moved to its **HALL**, with live boards showing who is waiting.

![](proving-grounds.png)

Four grounds, four fights:

**THE PROVING GROUNDS** — warm up and settle scores.
**RANKED BATTLE** — **1v1**, **2v2** or **3v3** for your rank.
**LAST STANDING** — choose **2 to 12** players, last one alive wins.
**CLAN BATTLE** — **2v2** or **3v3**, clan against clan.

Public Ranked queues never refuse a fight because somebody's rating is too far away. Rating picks the **FAIREST SPLIT** from the players already waiting; it never leaves a full queue standing still.

Want your own room? Switch to **INVITE ONLY**, invite both sides and start as soon as each side has somebody — even for an uneven fight such as **1v2**.

The queue stays visible after the menu closes: a boss bar, action bar, hall boards and server callout keep saying who is waiting and how many more are needed.

![](pvp-island-aerial.png)

Every arena carries a real **WORLD BORDER**, and **LAST STANDING**'s shrinks with how many are still fighting. **ENDER PEARLS** work inside every ring. Every match ends with a full **REPORT** — who you beat, what you dealt.

**KEEP INVENTORY** now covers the entire PvP gamemode — queues, lobbies, rings and spectators — including delayed End Crystal deaths after a match result.

Accepted opponents can finish their fight even when their clans are allied or open-world combat is paused. The exemption belongs only to that exact fight.

Private duels still return both players to the blocks they left. Queue fights return everybody to the PvP lobby for the next round.

## Under The Hood

![](optimization-banner.png)

⚡ We followed the work that repeats for every tick, player, packet and page load — then removed the repeats without changing what players see or earn.

### 🐉 Build The Island, Not The Empty Air

Building the Dragon arena fell from **2.13 MILLION** block operations to about **244,000** — **88.5% LESS** work before the fight.

The Dragon's empty exit-portal scan is gone before a death and runs once every five seconds afterwards — **100% LESS** useless scanning before the kill and **80% LESS** afterwards.

### 📊 Refresh What Changed

An active auto-seller used to provoke a complete leaderboard rebuild every **2 SECONDS**. It is capped at once every **30 SECONDS** now — up to **93.3% FEWER** full passes.

At **100 PLAYERS**, an unchanged tab refresh could broadcast **10,000** player-info packets. Unchanged rows now send **ZERO** — **100% OF THAT DUPLICATE TRAFFIC REMOVED**.

Spawn hologram refreshes used to destroy and respawn every line. An unchanged line now creates **0 ENTITIES** — **100% LESS ENTITY CHURN** for the lines that did not move.

Crate-odds saves now arrive in batches instead of one write at a time.
Cosmetic viewers are found once per tick instead of once per cosmetic.
Sidebar refresh requests that land together collapse into one pass.

### 🌐 Make The Pages Snap In

Every image on the website is now **95% SMALLER** to download — the pages just snap in.

Every hot server task also reports its real running time now, so the next slowdown has nowhere to hide.

*You will not see the work. You will feel the missing lag.*
