## Bedrock Is Back

**Minecraft Bedrock 1.26.45** is ready to join!
The proxy now speaks the latest Bedrock protocol, so the **Outdated Geyser Proxy** wall is gone.
Java and Bedrock players meet on the same server again.

*Same world. Newest client. No detour.*

## More Amethyst

The last update brought Amethyst into the world.
Now it arrives **far more often** — and the hunt starts at a distance worth travelling.

### 📦 Airdrops Within Reach

**Common** and **Rare** Airdrops now land **1,000-2,000 blocks** from **0, 0**.
**Legendary** drops land **5,000-10,000 blocks** out.
**Mythic** drops own the wilds from **10,000-25,000 blocks**.
Every announcement still gives you the **rarity, world, and exact coordinates**.

![](airdrop-garrison.png)

*The easy drops are closer. The dangerous ones still make you earn the trip.*

### 🟪 Events Every 15-30 Minutes

An **Airdrop** or **Huge Amethyst Block** now starts **15-30 minutes** after the last one ends.
They alternate, so one event never buries another.

![](huge-amethyst-block.png)

### ⏳ The Countdown Decides

Guards can no longer claim an Airdrop while its timer is still running.
If players do not claim it, the drop stays alive until the real countdown reaches zero.

*No more phantom victory for the garrison.*

## Cleaner Events

### ⚡ One Boost Bar

Stacked server boosts now share **one boss bar**.
You will see a clean line such as **4x Keys • 2x Airdrops** instead of bars covering the screen.

### 💤 AFK Means Safe From Shoving

AFK players are now **uncollidable**.
Nobody can push an idle player off a ledge while they are away.

### 🛒 Amethyst In Stock

When the rotating shop puts an **Amethyst item** in stock, the whole server hears about it.
No silent restocks. No finding out after it is gone.

## Changes

The public **Leaderboards** page is live again with current player and clan standings.

*Less waiting. Less clutter. More reasons to leave the base.*
