## Crates & Cosmetics

**v3.0** is here, and it is the biggest update the SMP has had.
Open **CRATES**, collect **COSMETICS**, and take them off other players.
Griefing is now officially **fair game**.

*Nothing you own is safe. That is the point.*

## Featuring

### 🎁 Mysterious Crates

`/crate` opens the brand-new crate system!
Earn **1 Mysterious Crate Key** for every hour you are online.
**AFK time counts**, and your progress is saved.
**1 key = 1 crate**, with no daily opening limit.
Run `/crate odds` for the exact chance on every reward.
Keys are **tradable**, so you can buy your luck from someone else.

*Turn the next-key timer off in `/settings` if it bothers you.*

![](crates-menu.png)

![](crate-opening.png)

Nothing is hidden. `/crate odds` lays out the whole table, every reward and every
chance, before you spend a single key.

![](crate-odds.png)

### ✨ Cosmetics

`/wardrobe` adds **18 cosmetics** across **auras**, **trails** and **kill effects**.
Plus one **secret** cosmetic that nobody has found yet.
Equip, unequip and manage every one of them from your wardrobe.
Sell them on through the **Auction House**.
Each one carries a real **in existence** count, so you know exactly how rare yours is.

![](wardrobe.png)

![](ember-trail.png)

And one of them nobody has opened yet. A black silhouette, no name, no rarity,
**in existence: 0**.

![](secret-cosmetic.png)

### 💀 They Can Be Stolen

Here is the catch.

**Killing another player steals their equipped cosmetics.**
That is the *only* way a cosmetic is ever lost.
Wear the rare one and you are a walking target.

*Wear it anyway.*

### 🗡️ Trophy Heads

Kill another player and their **head** drops as a trophy.
The same pairing only produces one every **24 hours**, so you cannot farm your friends for a wall of skulls.

## The World

### 🏴 Griefing Is Allowed

Player bases, farms, animals and clan builds are all **fair targets**.
Claimed or not. Occupied or not.
**Spawn** and designated **server builds** are the only exceptions.

This is deliberate. A world where anything can be lost is a world worth **defending** — it gives clans a reason to fortify and everyone a reason to log in and find out what changed overnight.

*Build accordingly.*

## Quality of Life

### ⚡ Auto Sell

`/autosell` sells eligible items **the moment they enter your inventory**.

> **It stops when 64 or more item entities are near you.** That is a deliberate
> guard against server lag, not a bug. The realistic use is standing on a hopper
> platform and clearing your farm's output quickly.

### 🛒 Auto Buy

`/shop` now supports **automatic purchases** on a **1 to 30 second** timer.
`/autobuy` shows what is running, and `/autobuy stop` ends it.

### ⚙️ Settings

`/settings` has been rebuilt into a **full interactive menu**.
Chat, notifications, PvP messages, cosmetics, privacy, scoreboard sections and the crate timer — all from one place.

*Requires **1.21.6 or newer**.*

![](settings.png)

### 🏪 Shop Changes

**Buy Full Inventory** — grab a full inventory of an item in one click.
**New items** — apples, string, dried kelp blocks and stripped bamboo blocks.

## Changes

### 🔧 Under the Hood

The server moved from **Paper 1.21.1** to **1.21.11**.
Hoppers now transfer items **60% faster**.
**Litematica Printer** should no longer be flagged by the anticheat.
The **Java resource pack** is fixed.
WorldEdit, WorldGuard and Geyser are all updated.

### 🐛 Fixes

Fixed oversized stacks coming out of bulk purchases.
Fixed scoreboard information being pushed off screen.
Clan boosts now display properly in the tab list.
**Extra Hearts** now shows your real total, including clan bonuses.
Improved stability across the **Auction House**, **bounties**, **clans** and the **economy**.
