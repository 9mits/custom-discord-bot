## Crates & Cosmetics

**v3.0** is here!
Open **Mysterious Crates** every hour!
Collect **18 cosmetics** — or steal them from somebody else!
And from now on, griefing is **fair game!**

*Nothing you own is safe. Perfect.*

## Featuring

### 🎁 Mysterious Crates

`/crate` opens the brand-new crate system!
A **Mysterious Crate Key** drops for every hour you are online!
**AFK counts**, progress saves, and the keys are **tradable**.
**1 key = 1 crate.** Open as many as you can earn!
Want the exact chances? `/crate odds` reveals every reward before you roll.
Don't want the next-key timer? Switch it off in `/settings`.

*Lucky hour?*

![](crate-opening.png)

Every reward. Every chance. **Nothing hidden!**

### 💀 They Can Be Stolen

See a cosmetic you want?
**Kill the player wearing it!**
Their equipped cosmetics become yours.
It is the **only** way a cosmetic can ever be lost!

*Dress expensive. Run fast.*

### 🗡️ Trophy Heads

Drop another player and their **head** drops as a trophy!
The same pairing can only make one every **24 hours**.

*Your friends are not an infinite skull farm. Sorry.*

## The World

### 🏴 Griefing Is Allowed

**Griefing is now allowed!**
Bases. Farms. Animals. Clan builds. All **fair targets**.
Claimed or not. Occupied or not.
Only **spawn** and designated **server builds** are safe.
The world has something worth **defending** again!

*Start building walls.*

## Quality of Life

### ⚡ Auto Sell

Eligible items hit your inventory. `/autosell` turns them into money **instantly!**
Stand on a hopper platform and tear through your farm output.
It pauses when **64+ dropped items** are nearby to protect the server.

### 🛒 Auto Buy

Set `/shop` to buy again every **1 to 30 seconds!**
Check the loop with `/autobuy`.
Stop it anytime with `/autobuy stop`.

### ⚙️ Settings

`/settings` is now a **full interactive menu!**
Chat. Notifications. PvP. Cosmetics. Privacy. Scoreboard. Crate timer.
All in one place!

*Bring **1.21.6 or newer**.*

![](settings.png)

### 🏪 Shop Changes

**Buy Full Inventory** grabs a full inventory in one click!
**New items:** apples, string, dried kelp blocks, and stripped bamboo blocks.
