## Spend Them Now

Last update ended with one instruction: **do not spend everything.**

This is what you were saving for.

The **Limited Amethyst Crate** is open, it costs **2 keys** a pull, and it has a
**countdown printed above it**. When that countdown reaches zero the crate leaves,
and every reward that only ever lived inside it leaves with it.

*Everyone who banked keys last week is about to feel very clever.*

![](spawn-crates.png)

## The Amethyst Crate

### 💜 Two Keys, And A Clock

The Default Crate is not going anywhere. **1 key, same as always.**

The Amethyst Crate sits beside it at **2 keys** a pull, and it is the first crate
on this server with an expiry. The hologram counts down in real **days, hours,
minutes and seconds** — not "soon", not "limited time only", an actual number you
can plan around.

Its reward table is **entirely its own**. Amethyst gear, amethyst cosmetics, and
three secrets that exist nowhere else.

*When it goes, it goes. There is no second run.*

### 🎰 Open Three At A Time

Ten pulls used to be ten clicks.

Flip **Open Three At A Time** in the crate menu and every pull opens **three crates
at once** — three reels, three reveals, one click.

It charges three pulls, because it is three pulls: **3 keys** on the Default Crate,
**6 keys** on the Amethyst, **3 Shards** on the Shard Crate.

![](open-three.png)

![](opening-3x.png)

### 🗑️ Auto Trash

Some rewards you will never want. Now you can say so.

Add anything to **Auto Trash** and the crate stops giving it to you — the roll still
happens, the reward is simply binned before it reaches your inventory. Pick exactly
which ones, change your mind whenever.

*Nobody needs their fortieth stack of oak.*

![](auto-trash.png)

![](auto-trash-picker.png)

### 📊 Its Own Odds Page

`/crate odds` now covers **each crate separately**, because each crate is a
different table.

Every reward, every exact chance, before you spend anything.

![](amethyst-odds.png)

## Amethyst Gear

![](amethyst-tools.png)

The three digging tools are the best pulls in the crate, and they share three rules.

They are **unbreakable**. They ship at **Efficiency V**, so you never spend an anvil
bringing them up to the speed their abilities already imply. And they run for
**24 hours from the first time you use one** — not from the moment you win it.

*The clock does not start until you do. Win one on Monday, spend it on Saturday.*

### ⛏️ Amethyst Pickaxe

Mines **3x3**, and **smelts every drop as it breaks**.

Iron ore comes out as ingots. Gold comes out as ingots. Cobblestone comes out as
stone. No furnace, no fuel, no second trip home.

### 🪓 Amethyst Axe

Fells an **entire tree** in one swing — up to **256 connected logs**.

*Bring a bigger inventory than you think you need.*

### 🥄 Amethyst Shovel

Clears a **3x3 plane** of everything a shovel is for.

Paths, foundations, and the gravel wall you have been pretending is not there.

### 🛡️ Amethyst Shield

![](amethyst-shield-totem.png)

**Crystal Guard**, **Reflect**, and **projectile bounce** — arrows come off it and
go back where they came from.

Block enough hits in a row and it answers with a **Guard Burst**.

*Turtling is now an offensive strategy.*

### 🗿 Amethyst Totem

It saves you from death, and then it **detonates**.

Every harmful effect on you is **cleansed**. You get **Regeneration**,
**Resistance**, and a **10-heart crystal shell** on top of your real health. Everything
standing near you is **thrown off its feet**.

**One use.** It is consumed the moment it fires, exactly like the totem it is based on.

*Dying has never been this theatrical.*

## Shards

![](key-and-shard.png)

### 🔷 The Shard Crate

**Shards** are the rarest currency on the server.

You cannot buy one. You cannot trade for one. They drop — from Amethyst Airdrops,
from the top of the online reward ladder at **1 in 5,000** — and that is the only
way they exist.

**One Shard opens the Shard Crate**, which has its own table, and unlike the
Amethyst Crate it is **not on a timer**.

![](shard-crate.png)

![](shard-odds.png)

*If you are holding one, you already got lucky once.*

## The World Fights Back

Amethyst does not only live in a crate. It **lands in the world**, and it does not
come quietly.

### 🟪 Huge Amethyst Block

A **Huge Amethyst Block** drops into the overworld with **2,750 HP** and a
**30 minute** timer over its head.

Mine it. Everyone mine it. It is deliberately far too big for one player — the
timer runs out long before a single pickaxe gets through it.

![](huge-amethyst-block.png)

Break the core before the clock does and it **shatters**, paying out to everybody
who helped.

![](amethyst-core-shattered.png)

*Bring friends. Bring pickaxes. Bring both.*

### 📦 Amethyst Airdrops

**Amethyst Airdrops** crash down across the map in rising rarities, all the way up
to **Mythic**.

Each one lands as a crystal fortress with the loot sealed in the middle, and the
rarity decides what is inside.

![](amethyst-airdrop.png)

![](mythic-airdrop-loot.png)

Twelve come out of the Amethyst Crate. Nine are locked behind leaderboard positions.

And one of them is not going to be listed anywhere.

### 💎 The Amethyst Family

**Twelve** new cosmetics drop from the Amethyst Crate and nowhere else — auras,
trails and kill effects built entirely out of crystal.

**Amethyst Ascension** raises a living crystal crown.
**Geode Cathedral** grows rotating arches that chime and collapse into violet light.
**Airdrop Apotheosis** suspends a royal drop-crystal inside broken halos.

**Crystal Guillotine** drops a blade through your final blow.

### 🤫 Three More Secrets

Three of them are **secrets** — no name, no rarity, no description in the menu.

![](secret-cosmetics.png)

Nine more cosmetics are **not in any crate at all**.

**Gold** for first: Solar Imperium, Kingmaker's Wake, Golden Finality.
**Silver** for second: Argent Dominion, Moonlit Procession, Silver Reckoning.
**Bronze** for third: Bronze Vanguard, Conqueror's March, Bronze Cataclysm.

![](podium-cosmetics.png)

*They are worn evidence. Lose the position, lose the look.*

## Clans

### ⚔️ Crates Clan Battle

Clans can now be dropped into a **Crates Clan Battle**.

Every crate a member opens counts toward their clan. The battle runs on its own
timer, the standings update live, and the board at spawn shows the top ten the
whole way through.

![](clan-battle.png)

The podium is paid in **Shards** — the currency you cannot buy — to **every member**
of the clan:

**1st — 10 Shards each**
**2nd — 5 Shards each**
**3rd — 3 Shards each**

And the winning clan gets something nobody else can ever obtain. Every member is
minted **Galactic Conquest**, an aura that drops from **no crate at all** and exists
only as proof you won one of these.

![](galactic-conquest.png)

*A living galaxy and a stellar crown, orbiting whoever opened the most.*

### 🎨 97 Clan Icons

Your clan picks its own **icon**, from **97** of them.

Diamonds, netherite, nether stars, hearts of the sea, ender pearls, and 92 more.
It shows on the clan card, on the leaderboard, and beside your name.

Clan **warps** are managed from the same menu now — set, rename, remove, no
commands to memorise.

![](clan-menu.png)

## Spawn, Rebuilt

### 💜 It Is Amethyst Now

Spawn has been **completely rebuilt** around the update.

Amethyst paths, crystal clusters, glowing inlays and a purple-roofed hall over the
crates. The whole area was redesigned to match what the update actually is.

![](spawn-aerial.png)

### 🏛️ The Event Leaderboard House

There is a **new building** at spawn that exists purely to keep score.

Inside are the **event boards** — one room each, top ten on every wall.
**Most Amethyst Crates Opened.** **Most Amethyst Airdrops Opened.**

![](board-crates.png)

![](board-airdrops.png)

Every board reads from the same live server data as the site and Discord, so
walking in is the fastest way to find out whether you are still winning.

### 🛡️ 100x100, Protected

The area around spawn is now a **protected 100x100 region**.

**No PvP.** **No TNT.** **No creeper, ghast or wither damage.** **No hostile mobs
spawning at all.** A tighter core inside it also stops **fall damage** and **mob
damage** outright and heals you.

Inside the plugin's own barrier, mobs that spawn outside are **stopped at the edge**
rather than wandering in, and blocks cannot be broken or placed by anyone.

Amethyst event mobs are the deliberate exception — a garrison is supposed to be
dangerous.

*Spawn is finally spawn.*

## ⟁ The Unnamed

*Set down here as the crate-keepers tell it, and believed by nobody sensible.*

They say that among the crystal there is **one more**.

It carries no name. It carries no rarity. The ledger that records every cosmetic
ever minted lists it at **nothing in existence**, and the menu that describes all
things declines to describe it.

The keepers hold that it is not won but **attended** — that it counts, patiently,
and that its price is **five hundred thousand** turns of the crate. Roll that many
times and, it is written, the thing shall **consider its appearance**.

Consider. Not promise.

*No player has seen it. That is not evidence that it is not there.*
