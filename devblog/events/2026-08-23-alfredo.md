## Alfredo

He arrived at **spawn**, sixteen times the size of a normal zombie, wearing his name in letters as tall as he was.

Then he just stood there. Glowing. On fire. Waiting.

Everyone hit him at once.

*He did not hit back. He did not have to — he was the one holding the loot.*

![](alfredo.png)

## How It Works

### 👹 He Is Enormous

Alfredo spawns at **2,000 health** and the maximum size the game allows.
The sky turns over above him, lightning cracks around him, and the whole arena fills with colour.
His nameplate is scaled to match, so you can find him from anywhere.

### 🔑 He Pays As He Falls

Alfredo carries a fixed treasury of **keys** and **diamonds** and gives it up in **ten bursts** on the way down.
Every time his health crosses another notch, part of it erupts out of him.

Half his treasury is paid out during the fight.
**The other half is saved for the end.**

### 💎 The Finale

When Alfredo finally goes down, everything he was still carrying goes into the air at once.
Keys and diamonds rain across the arena in a fountain, and whatever the bursts never got round to dropping comes with it.

*Bring an empty inventory. You will need it.*

### ⚔️ No PvP

**PvP is switched off across the whole server** while Alfredo is up.
Nobody loses their haul to somebody standing behind them.
It comes back on the moment he falls.

## Event Details

**Ran:** Saturday 22 August 2026, 20:00 JST
**Location:** Spawn
**Requirements:** None. Turn up and swing.
**Rewards:** Mysterious Crate Keys and diamonds, split between ten bursts and one enormous finale

Alfredo takes no revenge, deals no damage and breaks nothing.
The only thing he threatens is your hotbar space.

*He is not gone. He is just resting.*
